Scan turns paper, receipts, forms, IDs, notes, books and existing PDFs into a searchable local document library on Android.

CAPTURE DOCUMENTS
• Scan multiple pages with automatic document detection and review
• Specialized modes for documents, receipts, IDs, business cards, books, whiteboards, forms, photos, notes and certificates
• Import existing PDFs and images
• Rapid capture for supported document workflows

FIND WHAT YOU SCANNED
• On-device OCR with local full-text search
• Exact phrase and prefix search
• Jump directly to pages containing a match
• Export searchable PDFs with an invisible text layer

FIX PAGES AFTER CAPTURE
• Reorder, rotate, duplicate or replace pages
• Perspective crop and document enhancement
• Smart cleanup for marks, shadows and other distractions
• Edit recognized text
• Annotate, highlight, redact and sign
• Detect and fill form fields

ORGANIZE A REAL DOCUMENT LIBRARY
• Nested folders and reusable tags
• Favorites, archive and Trash
• Smart collections such as Recent, Unfiled and Needs Review
• Document types, local classification suggestions and bulk actions
• Reusable automation and processing presets

EXPORT USEFUL RESULTS
• Searchable PDF export
• Original, High, Balanced and Small quality presets
• Merge documents and extract page ranges
• Plain-text, CSV, JSON and XLSX exports where applicable
• AES-256 password-protected PDF copies
• Save through Android document providers without broad storage permission

LOCAL-FIRST BY DESIGN
Scan does not require an account or subscription. Your document library, OCR results and editing data remain on your device in app-private storage.

The app itself does not request the INTERNET permission. Scanner capture is provided by Google Play services through ML Kit. The document scanner processes scanned image content on-device; Google documents that ML Kit may collect limited device, app, performance and usage diagnostics to maintain and improve its SDKs.

ENCRYPTED RECOVERY
Create an encrypted portable backup of Scan data for recovery after reinstall or app-data loss. Secure-vault controls, privacy-oriented PDF export and local security auditing are available for sensitive workflows.

Scan is open source and built around a simple goal: paper should become searchable without forcing your documents into an account or cloud workflow.
